## Business problem

Existing servicing customers may not know when their Freedom Account contains a
personalized offer that could help lower their monthly payment. Today, customers
must discover the offer by signing in and navigating to it.

## Requested capability

Let an authenticated existing customer enroll in rate-savings alerts. When the
existing offer service indicates that a personalized offer is available, notify
the customer by email and SMS and direct them to the authenticated offer details.

## Intended outcome

Improve customer awareness of relevant personalized offers and increase
qualified engagement with offer details.

No numeric target has been approved.

## Initial scope

- Enrollment inside Freedom Account.
- Email and SMS alert delivery.
- Secure navigation back to authenticated offer details.
- Enrollment, delivery, and engagement reporting.

## Known constraints

- Use the existing personalized-offer decision.
- Do not change pricing, eligibility, savings, or underwriting calculations.
- Do not place personalized financial details in an alert.
- Do not store customer or production data in the requirements repository.

## Initially impacted groups

- Freedom Account product and delivery team.
- Notifications team.
- Existing offer-service owner.
- Legal/Compliance reviewer.
- Analytics partner.

The sponsor approved detailed discovery. This is not final BRD scope signoff,
customer-language approval, or delivery authorization.
