# Synthetic Discovery Notes

These notes represent a short discovery session held after the initiative
entered the prioritized backlog.

## Confirmed by the product owner

- Phase one serves authenticated existing customers.
- The initiative does not create a new offer or change an offer decision.
- The alert should direct the customer to Freedom Account for all personalized
  details.
- The product owner requested email and SMS at launch.
- Customers must be able to leave the alert program.

## Confirmed by the offer-service owner

- The existing service can indicate that a personalized offer is present.
- This initiative must treat that response as authoritative.
- The requirements package must not reinterpret the offer response.

## Requested by Analytics

- Measure enrollment completion.
- Measure successful alert delivery.
- Measure authenticated visits to offer details originating from an alert.
- Success thresholds remain to be decided by the product owner and sponsor.

## Not yet decided

- Maximum alert frequency and repeat-alert suppression window.
- Whether enrollment expires.
- Phase-one success thresholds.
- Final customer-facing language.
- Operational owner for failed deliveries.

## Product-review checkpoint

Before drafting the BRD, identify conflicts between these notes and the two team
context repositories. Do not resolve conflicts without the product owner's
decision.
